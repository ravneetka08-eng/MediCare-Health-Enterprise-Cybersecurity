# Data Classification Standard

## Purpose

This standard defines the information-classification model used by
MediCare Health Australia.

Information classification helps determine the security controls
required to protect information throughout its lifecycle.

The organisation and all information used in the technical laboratory
are fictional.

---

# Classification Levels

## CLASS-01 — Public

Information approved for public disclosure.

Examples:

- public website content;
- published service information;
- public contact details;
- approved marketing material.

### Security Requirements

Confidentiality: Low

Integrity: Medium

Availability: Medium

Access:
Public access permitted.

---

## CLASS-02 — Internal

Information intended for organisational use but where unauthorised
disclosure would normally create limited harm.

Examples:

- internal procedures;
- general internal communications;
- training material;
- non-sensitive operational documentation.

### Security Requirements

Confidentiality: Medium

Integrity: Medium

Availability: Medium

Access:
Authorised workforce members.

---

## CLASS-03 — Confidential

Sensitive organisational information where unauthorised disclosure,
modification or loss could materially affect the organisation or
individuals.

Examples:

- employee records;
- financial information;
- security documentation;
- vulnerability reports;
- security logs;
- internal architecture information.

### Security Requirements

Confidentiality: High

Integrity: High

Availability: Medium to High

Access:
Role-based and business-need access.

---

## CLASS-04 — Restricted

Highly sensitive information requiring the strongest level of
protection within this project.

Examples:

- patient clinical information;
- patient identifying information;
- authentication secrets;
- privileged credentials;
- selected backup information;
- sensitive incident evidence.

### Security Requirements

Confidentiality: Critical

Integrity: Critical

Availability: High to Critical depending on the system.

Access:
Strictly limited according to authorised role and business need.

---

# General Handling Requirements

## Public

May be shared externally when approved.

## Internal

Must remain within approved organisational systems unless authorised.

## Confidential

Should use:

- authenticated access;
- least privilege;
- appropriate encryption;
- access logging;
- approved storage;
- controlled sharing.

## Restricted

Should use stronger protections including:

- strict role-based access control;
- least privilege;
- strong authentication;
- encryption where appropriate;
- access monitoring;
- restricted administrative access;
- controlled backup;
- security logging;
- periodic access review;
- incident-response procedures.

---

# Laboratory Data Requirement

No real patient or employee information will be used.

All healthcare and workforce information used during technical testing
will be synthetically generated.

Synthetic information will still be treated according to the
classification assigned to the information it represents.

This allows security controls to be tested realistically without
processing real sensitive information.
