# 1. Purpose

This dashboard provides management-level visibility of cybersecurity
risk across the fictional healthcare enterprise.

It is intended to support:

- prioritisation;
- risk escalation;
- treatment oversight;
- exception management;
- identification of overdue actions;
- monitoring of risk exposure;
- management decision-making.

This dashboard summarises the underlying risk register.

It does not replace detailed risk records.

---

# 2. Executive Risk Position

## Current Baseline

The project currently contains:

- 12 identified enterprise cybersecurity risks;
- 3 Critical inherent risks;
- 9 High inherent risks;
- 0 Medium inherent risks;
- 0 Low inherent risks.

Residual risk ratings remain pending for risks where relevant control
implementation and operating effectiveness have not yet been
validated.

This is intentional.

The dashboard must not report artificial risk reduction before
supporting evidence exists.

## Clinical Information Protection

Relevant risks:

- R-001
- R-005
- R-011

Management concern:

---

## Cyber Resilience

Relevant risks:

- R-003
- R-004
- R-009

Management concern:

Endpoint compromise, unresolved vulnerabilities and ineffective
recovery capability could combine to increase the impact of a
significant cyber incident.

---

## Detection and Response

Relevant risk:

- R-008

Related risks:

- R-001
- R-003
- R-010

Management concern:

risk scenarios.

# 14. Reporting Limitations

This dashboard represents a fictional healthcare cybersecurity
environment.

It does not represent the cybersecurity posture, risk profile or
control maturity of any real healthcare organisation.

Risk counts are intentionally scoped to material scenarios selected
for this portfolio project and should not be interpreted as a
complete enterprise risk inventory.
