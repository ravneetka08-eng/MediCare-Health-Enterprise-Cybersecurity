# Crown-Jewel Assets

## Purpose

This document identifies information and technology assets whose
compromise could create significant operational, privacy, security
or clinical consequences for the fictional healthcare organisation.

---

## CJ-001 — Patient Clinical Information

Related assets:

- AST-016 Patient Clinical Records
- AST-017 Patient Identity Information

Security priorities:

- Confidentiality: Critical
- Integrity: Critical
- Availability: Critical

Potential impact:

- privacy harm;
- unauthorised disclosure;
- inappropriate modification of clinical information;
- disruption to clinical decision-making;
- regulatory consequences;
- loss of organisational trust.

---

## CJ-002 — Enterprise Identity Infrastructure

Related assets:

- AST-001 Active Directory
- AST-002 DC01
- AST-005 Enterprise Identity Accounts
- AST-006 Privileged Administrator Accounts

Security priorities:

- Confidentiality: High
- Integrity: Critical
- Availability: Critical

Potential impact:

Compromise of enterprise identity infrastructure could allow an
attacker to gain elevated access, impersonate users, move between
systems and interfere with security controls.

---

## CJ-003 — Critical Clinical Systems

Related assets:

- AST-010 Electronic Medical Record System
- AST-011 Patient Administration System
- AST-012 Pathology / Laboratory System
- AST-013 Pharmacy / Medication System
- AST-014 Medical Imaging / PACS

Security priorities:

- Confidentiality: Critical
- Integrity: Critical
- Availability: Critical

Potential impact:

Disruption or unauthorised modification could affect healthcare
operations and access to clinical information.

---

## CJ-004 — Backup and Recovery Capability

Related assets:

- AST-021 Backup Data
- AST-028 Backup Repository
- AST-029 Recovery Procedure

Security priorities:

- Confidentiality: High
- Integrity: Critical
- Availability: Critical

Potential impact:

Loss or compromise of backup capability could prevent effective
recovery following ransomware, system failure or destructive
cybersecurity incidents.

---

## CJ-005 — Security Monitoring Evidence

Related assets:

- AST-020 Security Logs
- AST-022 Wazuh Security Platform
- AST-035 AWS CloudTrail

Security priorities:

- Confidentiality: Medium
- Integrity: Critical
- Availability: High

Potential impact:

An attacker who can delete or modify security evidence may reduce
the organisation's ability to detect, investigate and reconstruct
security incidents.
