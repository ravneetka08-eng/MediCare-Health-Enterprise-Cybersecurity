# Enterprise Identity and Access Roles

## Purpose

This document defines representative workforce roles used to design
and test Role-Based Access Control within the project.

These identities are fictional.

---

# ROLE-001 — Reception Officer

Representative User:

reception.user

Business Responsibilities:

- patient registration;
- appointment administration;
- demographic updates.

Permitted Access:

- patient demographic information;
- appointment information;
- limited patient administration functions.

Not Normally Required:

- detailed clinical notes;
- security systems;
- domain administration;
- backup administration;
- AWS administration.

---

# ROLE-002 — Doctor

Representative User:

doctor.user

Business Responsibilities:

- patient assessment;
- clinical decision-making;
- clinical documentation.

Permitted Access:

- authorised patient clinical records;
- clinical history;
- pathology results;
- relevant medication information.

Not Normally Required:

- domain administration;
- security administration;
- backup administration;
- unrestricted HR records.

---

# ROLE-003 — Nurse

Representative User:

nurse.user

Business Responsibilities:

- patient care;
- clinical documentation;
- authorised clinical workflows.

Permitted Access:

- appropriate patient clinical information;
- selected care documentation.

Access may differ from the Doctor role according to business need.

---

# ROLE-004 — IT Support

Representative User:

it.support

Business Responsibilities:

- endpoint support;
- approved account support;
- infrastructure troubleshooting.

Permitted Access:

- approved IT administration functions.

Important Principle:

Technical administration of a system does not automatically imply
a business requirement to view patient clinical content.

---

# ROLE-005 — Privileged Administrator

Representative User:

adm.admin

Business Responsibilities:

- Active Directory administration;
- server administration;
- approved privileged changes.

Security Requirements:

- separate privileged identity;
- no routine email/web browsing using the privileged account;
- enhanced monitoring;
- least privilege;
- restricted use.

---

# ROLE-006 — Security Analyst

Representative User:

sec.analyst

Business Responsibilities:

- security monitoring;
- alert investigation;
- vulnerability management;
- incident-response support;
- security-control testing.

Permitted Access:

- security telemetry;
- approved security tools;
- incident evidence according to business need.

---

# ROLE-007 — Former Employee

Representative User:

former.employee

Purpose:

This identity will support Joiner, Mover and Leaver testing.

Expected State:

Disabled.

The account will be used to verify that terminated users cannot
continue authenticating to organisational systems.

---

# Access-Control Principle

Permissions should be assigned primarily according to business role
rather than individually granting broad access to each user.

Representative model:

User
  |
  v
Role / Security Group
  |
  v
Permission
  |
  v
Business Resource

Example:

doctor.user
     |
     v
GG-Clinical-Doctors
     |
     v
Clinical-Records-Access
     |
     v
Synthetic Clinical Information
