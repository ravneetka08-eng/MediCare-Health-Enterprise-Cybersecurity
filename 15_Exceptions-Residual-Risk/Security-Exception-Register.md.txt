---

## Security Requirement

Enterprise security standards require supported systems to receive
applicable security updates within defined vulnerability-remediation
timeframes.

---

## Non-Compliance

The representative clinical imaging workstation cannot currently
receive the required operating-system security update.

---

## Technical Reason

The workstation operates vendor-dependent clinical imaging software.

The required operating-system update has not been certified within
the fictional scenario by the medical-technology vendor.

Applying an unsupported update may create:

- application incompatibility;
- device instability;
- vendor-support implications;
- clinical service interruption.

---

## Cybersecurity Exposure

Leaving the vulnerability unresolved may increase the likelihood of:

- system exploitation;
- malicious code execution;
- credential compromise;
- lateral movement;
- service disruption.

---

## Business / Clinical Constraint

Immediate remediation through normal patching is not considered
operationally acceptable until application compatibility and clinical
service impact have been appropriately assessed.

The existence of a clinical constraint does not eliminate the cyber
risk.

---

## Inherent Risk

Likelihood:

4 — Likely

Impact:

4 — Major

Score:

16 — High

---

## Preferred Control

Install the approved security update within the standard vulnerability
remediation timeframe.

Current State:

Not currently achievable within the scenario.

---

## Compensating Controls

Until permanent remediation is available, the following compensating
controls are proposed.

### CC-EX001-01 — Dedicated Network Segment

Place the affected workstation within a restricted Medical / IoT
network segment.

Purpose:

Reduce unnecessary connectivity and lateral-movement opportunities.

---

### CC-EX001-02 — Restrictive Firewall Policy

Permit only explicitly required communication between the imaging
workstation and authorised systems.

Default behaviour:

Deny unnecessary network communication.

---

### CC-EX001-03 — Internet Restriction

Prevent direct general Internet access unless technically required
and formally justified.

---

### CC-EX001-04 — Restricted Administrative Access

Limit administrative access to authorised technical personnel.

---

### CC-EX001-05 — Enhanced Security Monitoring

Monitor available network and host telemetry for suspicious activity.

Where host-based monitoring cannot be installed because of vendor
restrictions, network-based monitoring should be considered.

---

### CC-EX001-06 — Application Allowlisting / Execution Restriction

Where technically supported and vendor-approved, restrict execution
to authorised software.

---

### CC-EX001-07 — Backup / Recovery Capability

Ensure required system configuration and supported data are included
within appropriate recovery arrangements.

---

### CC-EX001-08 — Vendor Engagement

Track vendor guidance concerning:

- security updates;
- supported software versions;
- remediation availability;
- upgrade path.

---

### CC-EX001-09 — Replacement Roadmap

Develop a funded or approved plan to:

- upgrade;
- replace; or
- decommission

the unsupported technology.

Compensating controls are not considered the permanent target state.

---

## Control Limitations

The compensating controls reduce exposure but do not remove the
underlying vulnerability.

Residual exposure may remain because:

- the vulnerable software remains present;
- exploitation may still be possible through permitted communication;
- monitoring may detect rather than prevent activity;
- operational requirements may prevent complete isolation.

---

## Residual Risk

Status:

To Be Assessed following validation of compensating controls.

Residual risk must not automatically be classified as Low merely
because compensating controls have been documented.

---

## Evidence Required

Representative evidence may include:

- network architecture;
- VLAN configuration;
- firewall rule review;
- connectivity test;
- blocked connectivity test;
- administrative access configuration;
- monitoring events;
- vendor correspondence simulation;
- replacement plan;
- risk approval.

---

## Long-Term Remediation

Preferred long-term outcome:

1. obtain vendor-supported remediation;
2. validate compatibility;
3. implement through controlled change;
4. perform post-change testing;
5. rescan/reassess;
6. remove unnecessary compensating controls where appropriate;
7. close the exception.

Alternative:

Replace or decommission the unsupported system.

---

## Approval Decision

Current State:

Pending.

The exception remains a project scenario until the associated risk,
controls and evidence are developed.

---

## Closure Criteria

EX-001 may close when:

- the vulnerability is permanently remediated; or
- the affected technology is replaced/decommissioned;
- technical validation confirms the target state;
- R-011 is reassessed;
- the Risk Owner approves closure.
