# 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how deviations from approved cybersecurity
requirements are identified, assessed, approved, monitored and
closed.

A security exception does not mean that a security requirement no
longer matters.

It means that the required control cannot currently be implemented
as intended and the resulting exposure must be explicitly managed.

---

# 2. Example Exception Conditions

Exceptions may arise because of:

- unsupported legacy systems;
- medical-device restrictions;
- vendor limitations;
- application compatibility;
- operational dependencies;
- clinical availability requirements;
- technical constraints;
- temporary project requirements;
- delayed replacement programs.

Cost or inconvenience alone does not automatically justify an
exception.
